## Project Overview

The Supermarket Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based project developed to analyze and visualize supermarket sales data. The dashboard provides a clear overview of sales performance, product performance, customer ratings, payment methods, and sales across different cities.

The project involved cleaning and organizing the raw sales data, followed by data analysis using Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, slicers, and interactive visualizations. The dashboard helps users easily explore the data and identify important sales patterns and trends.

The main objective of this project is to transform raw supermarket sales data into meaningful insights that can support better understanding of sales performance and business operations.

### Key Areas Analyzed

* Total Sales and Total Quantity
* Total Income and Average Customer Rating
* Total Number of Invoices
* Sales performance by city
* Sales by product line
* Payment method analysis
* Customer rating analysis
* Sales trends and overall business performance
